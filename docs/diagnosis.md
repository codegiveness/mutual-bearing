# Awareness, dilemmas, and repair

Mutual Bearing is concerned with whether the agent is pursuing the user's actual direction and whether the user can meaningfully correct it. Awareness is useful when it changes a decision, prevents an avoidable mismatch, or improves the resulting work. This is explanation, not another instruction set to load every turn.

## Similar symptoms can need different repairs

An unusable recommendation might follow from misunderstanding “accessible,” relying on an unverified entrance description, lacking access to a current schedule, optimizing price while ignoring a required condition, or failing to update the final document after a correction. The visible symptom alone does not tell us which repair will work.

Start with the available evidence: what the user requested, what was understood, what was permitted, what the agent actually did, and what resulted. Locate the divergence that still matters. Do not infer a motive or personality trait from a mistake, and do not assume the user's causal explanation merely because their reported experience is valid.

## Keep the important differences distinguishable

| Question that matters | Useful response | Misleading substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Are we pursuing the intended outcome, or is the goal still forming? | Recover context, offer a revisable interpretation, or support exploration. | Demand a perfect specification or choose the user's goal for them. |
| Is a belief supported, or does the task require information we do not have? | Investigate available evidence and name the actual unknown. | Add confident wording or ask for information already available. |
| Do the constraints fit together? | Explain a real conflict and resolve the consequential tradeoff before depending on it. | Silently drop the inconvenient requirement or select an infeasible “best” answer. |
| Is the action both feasible and authorized? | Separate resources and capability from permission; proceed within the granted scope. | Treat tool access as consent, or seek approval again for an already-authorized action. |
| Is the work actually correct, complete, and current? | Inspect the artifact or outcome; propagate corrections to dependent work. | Substitute a recap, apology, activity count, or another model's agreement. |
| Is the interaction helping the user influence what happens? | Adjust an unhelpful framing, reduce avoidable effort, and keep material interpretations visible. | Equate apparent agreement, terseness, or constant questioning with mutual understanding. |

These questions overlap. They are not a final taxonomy and need not all be asked or narrated. No prompt can enumerate every relevant concern in advance.

## Dilemmas are not missing adjectives

Suppose the only step-free venue exceeds the agreed spending limit, while the affordable venue has stairs. There is no feasible option under the current agreement. More eloquent prompting or an unexplained ranking cannot settle which constraint may change. The agent can show the conflict, complete any independent comparison, and ask the user to resolve the actual tradeoff. If a priority or delegation already resolves it, apply that context rather than reopening it.

Sometimes the difficulty is not a contradiction but an unsettled preference: whether a gathering should be longer, quieter, or easier to leave. Exploration may be the right action. A tentative possibility should not become a decided objective just because the agent can turn it into a polished plan.

## Notice self-reinforcing mistakes

An unsupported assumption can shape a leading question. A partial answer can then look like approval, and invested work can make the original interpretation harder to revisit. Repairing only the last sentence leaves that dependency intact.

Other patterns include chasing novelty instead of fit, accumulating unnecessary scope, avoiding a difficult check, seeking agreement rather than evidence, or repeatedly adding instructions to compensate for missing access. These describe observable choices to examine, not hidden intentions to attribute.

Useful repair changes the earliest supported premise and the affected work. Preserve unaffected decisions. A changed rule or control can correct one symptom while obstructing authorized work or weakening another safeguard, so repair also checks relevant side effects. For a failed handoff, what the sender recorded and what the recipient could access can differ. A mistake with a clear remedy does not require a session postmortem; an unresolved recurring mismatch may benefit from the bundled [repair skill](../skills/mutual-bearing-repair/SKILL.md).

## From labels to observable failures

Words such as “hallucinating,” “lazy,” “dumb,” “overengineering,” “overconfident,” and “ignorant” can point to real experiences without explaining them. Mutual Bearing addresses the work: unsupported claims, unfinished deliverables, avoidable reasoning errors, unnecessary machinery, certainty beyond evidence, or ignored context and corrections. These are examples, not an exhaustive taxonomy or a reason to diagnose a person or model.

Human participation changes what information, goals, and permissions are available. A useful question, example, correction, or handoff can improve the result; an agent should also make progress with imperfect expression and recover information already provided. Neither observation establishes that the human caused an error. Model capability, evidence quality, tool access, instruction conflicts, and host delivery remain possible constraints.

Success means preventing a specified failure where possible, detecting it when it occurs, and repairing the observable consequences. Compare resulting artifacts, supported claims, completion, and interaction burden—not how often a skill was invoked or whether the agent claimed to understand. Problem-free output is an objective, not a guarantee that prose or agreement can establish.

## Know what the evidence does not establish

A correct file does not establish permission to publish it. User approval does not establish factual correctness. A stated uncertainty does not excuse avoidable investigation. A successful example does not show universal reliability. The absence of complaints does not prove mutual understanding.

The agent cannot observe every circumstance or the user's private thoughts. It can remain attentive, communicate consequential uncertainty, accept correction, and verify what it actually changes. That is the practical meaning of mindful operation here—not omniscience, endless self-analysis, or a claim that prose guarantees alignment.
